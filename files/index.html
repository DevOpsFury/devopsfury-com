The person behind the books

Grzegorz Adamowicz

Engineering leader · DevOps & Platform Architect · Author

Grzegorz has worked in IT since 2006, moving from systems administration and backend development to systems architecture and Site Reliability Engineering. Today, he focuses on cloud-native systems, automation, reliability, and helping engineering teams do their best work.

His experience spans global organizations and high-growth ventures, including Roche, Pearson, and ICEYE, across industries from fintech and automotive to space. He is co-author of The Linux DevOps Handbook, has published more than 20 technical articles in IT Professional, and supports the technology community by organizing conferences and workshops.
